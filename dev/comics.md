## Act 1
 ### Ch 1 - Paradigms (Square Wheels)
  Draw a single panel cartoon, black and white. Two peasants are struggling to pull a cart filled with spoked wagon wheels, round. 
  The four wagon wheels on the cart are square.
 ### Ch 2 - Title (Logic)
  Draw a four panel cartoon in black and white. Characters, smart son, mom, and dad. 
  Panel 1: “Mom, can Randal sleep over tomorrow?” Mom, “If it’s ok with your father.” 
  Panel 2: “Dad, can Randal sleep over tomorrow?” Dad, “If it’s ok with your mother.” 
  Panel 3: Smart son, looking stumped, no words. 
  Panel 4: smart son on phone with an open book on logic, “Randal, this isn’t as easy as it looks…”

 ### Ch 3 - Title (Two sticks)
  Draw a single panel cartoon, black and white. Two cavemen around a campfire. 
  The first one says, “You make it by rubbing two sticks together” The second responds, “Impossible! I invented common sense just last week.”

 ### Ch 4 - Title (Amber particles of grain)
  Draw a single panel cartoon, black and white. Two characters observing a Kansas wheat field, a light breeze waving the stalks. 
  First character, in awe of the beauty, “Amber waves of grain…”, The second, a wit, quips, “They are only waves until you look at them.”

  Draw a single panel cartoon, black and white. Irate customer holding a boomerang, “I want a refund, this boomerang doesn’t work.”

 ### Ch 12 - Contradiction (comic)
  Draw a multi-panel cartoon, black and white. Two friends have an argument about the validity of horoscopes. 
  Friend one, reading the horoscope in the daily paper, “It says you are going to have a fight with a friend tomorrow.” 
  Second friend, “Nope, not now, I can avoid it.” They argue back and forth, snide, insults, counter claims. Then silence. 
  Then, last panel, the first friend notices the date on the newspaper, “What? This is yesterday’s newspaper.”
