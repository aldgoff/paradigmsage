Appendix I – The Congruent Rules of Chess

The congruent rules of chess; dimension agnostic, in presentation order.

CONGRUENT RULE 1: A CHESS BOARD IS A REGULAR ARRAY OF REGULAR THINGS.	23
CONGRUENT RULE 2: UNIT CELLS (TILES) ALTERNATE BETWEEN TWO COLORS, TYPICALLY WHITE AND BLACK.	23
CONGRUENT RULE 3: UNRESTRICTED PIECES MAY MOVE THE FULL EXTENT OF THE BOARD.	23
CONGRUENT RULE 4: ROOKS ADVANCE ALONG RAYS WHICH CHANGE ONLY ONE COORDINATE.	25
CONGRUENT RULE 5: BISHOPS ADVANCE ALONG RAYS WHICH CHANGE TWO COORDINATES.	25
CONGRUENT RULE 6: CHESS REQUIRES A BASE PIECE FOR EVERY DIMENSION.	26
CONGRUENT RULE 7: EACH NEW BASE PIECE CAN REACH ONLY HALF AS MANY TILES AS THE PREVIOUS ONE.	26
CONGRUENT RULE 8: PIECE NAMES DERIVE LARGELY FROM A MEDIEVAL MOTIF.	26
CONGRUENT RULE 9: THE LENGTH OF A CHESS BOARD IS 8 TILES.	28
CONGRUENT RULE 10: A TILE MAY BE EMPTY OR CONTAIN ONE AND ONLY ONE PIECE.	28
CONGRUENT RULE 11: ALL BASE PIECES EXCEPT THE ROOK MAY BE COMBINED INTO A COMPOSITE PIECE.	29
CONGRUENT RULE 12: THE DESIGN OF EACH PIECE ALLUDES TO HOW IT MOVES.	31
CONGRUENT RULE 13: PIECES START IN TWO LINES OPPOSED ON 2ND HIGHEST ORDER FEATURE, PAWNS IN FRONT.	32
CONGRUENT RULE 14: WHITE KING ROOK STARTS ON A WHITE TILE.	32
CONGRUENT RULE 15: A PAWN’S PROMOTION TILE IS ANY OF THE OPPONENT’S HOME TILES (8,8).	34
CONGRUENT RULE 16: WHITE MOVES FIRST.	34
CONGRUENT RULE 17: BASE PIECES MOVE IN A SPACE ONE DIMENSION LESS THAN THE BOARD.	37
CONGRUENT RULE 18: A PIECE MAY ADVANCE ONLY AS LONG AS THE NEXT PERIMETER IS EMPTY.	37
CONGRUENT RULE 19: AN ADVANCEMENT MANIFOLD IS DEFINED BY N-1 RAYS OF THE SAME TYPE.	37
CONGRUENT RULE 20: A BASE PIECE CAN MOVE TO ANY TILE IT CAN REACH IN JUST TWO MOVES.	37
CONGRUENT RULE 21: AN ADVANCEMENT RECTANGLE IS COMPOSED OF TWO ADJACENT ADVANCEMENT SQUARES.	38
CONGRUENT RULE 22: A LINEAR MOVE IS LEGAL IF AT LEAST ONE ADVANCEMENT RECTANGLE IS EMPTY.	38
CONGRUENT RULE 23: A LINEAR MOVE OCCURS IN EVERY UNBLOCKED PLANE THAT INTERSECT ALONG IT.	38
CONGRUENT RULE 24: THE ROOK CAN REACH ALL THE TILES ON THE BOARD.	41
CONGRUENT RULE 25: PROJECTION OF THE 3D ROOK MOVE ONTO TWO DIMENSIONS YIELDS THE 2D MOVE.	44
CONGRUENT RULE 26: THE BISHOP CAN REACH HALF THE TILES ON THE BOARD.	53
CONGRUENT RULE 27: PROJECTION OF THE 3D BISHOP MOVE ONTO TWO DIMENSIONS YIELDS THE 2D MOVE.	60
CONGRUENT RULE 28: THE DUKE CAN REACH ONLY A QUARTER OF THE TILES ON THE BOARD.	71
CONGRUENT RULE 29: THE QUEEN IS THE SUM OF THE BASE PIECES.	101
CONGRUENT RULE 30: QUEENS START ON OPPOSITE COLORS OF THE HIGHEST BASE PIECE.	101
CONGRUENT RULE 31: THE KNIGHT DOES NOT ADVANCE, IT JUMPS; THUS, IT CANNOT BE BLOCKED.	118
CONGRUENT RULE 32: THE KNIGHT IS THE ONLY PIECE WHICH CAN ATTACK THE QUEEN WITHOUT A COUNTERATTACK.	118
CONGRUENT RULE 33: THE KNIGHT IS THE ANTI-QUEEN, RANGE LIMITED TO A 5N BOUNDARY, CANNOT BE BLOCKED.	118
CONGRUENT RULE 34: PROJECTION OF THE 3D KNIGHT MOVE ONTO A 2D SURFACE IS THE 2D KNIGHT MOVE.	118
CONGRUENT RULE 35: THE KNIGHT CHANGES ALL HIGHER ORDER BASE PIECE COLORS.	118
CONGRUENT RULE 36: THE PAWN ADVANCES LIKE A ROOK BUT CAPTURES LIKE THE OTHER BASE PIECES.	145
CONGRUENT RULE 37: THE PAWN ADVANCES OR CAPTURES TOWARD PROMOTION.	145
CONGRUENT RULE 38: PAWNS ARE LIMITED TO MOVING TO PERIMETER 1, WITH ONE EXCEPTION.	146
CONGRUENT RULE 39: ON THE FIRST MOVE ONLY, A PAWN MAY DOUBLE ADVANCE TO PERIMETER 2.	146
CONGRUENT RULE 40: PAWNS MAY ADVANCE ONLY IN A DOMINANT MANIFOLD.	146
CONGRUENT RULE 41: PAWNS PROMOTE AT THE OPPONENT’S HOME LINE; TO ANY MAJOR PIECE EXCEPT THE KING.	148
CONGRUENT RULE 42: THE RANGE OF A SHACKLED PAWN IS THE NUMBER OF PROMOTION TILES IN ITS MANIFOLD.	150
CONGRUENT RULE 43: PROMOTION DISTANCE IS THE MINIMUM NUMBER OF PAWN SINGLE ADVANCES REQUIRED IN A MINIMALLY DOMINANT MANIFOLD.	152
CONGRUENT RULE 44: FOR PAWNS, A QUADRANT WITH ANY TILES FARTHER FROM PROMOTION IS VIRTUAL.	153
CONGRUENT RULE 45: A VIRTUAL COMPONENT REDUCES A COMPOUND ADVANCEMENT MANIFOLD TO A SIMPLE ONE.	153
CONGRUENT RULE 46: VIRTUAL QUADRANTS DO NOT BLOCK.	153
CONGRUENT RULE 47: PAWN PROMOTIONS ARE IRREVERSIBLE.	168
CONGRUENT RULE 48: CHESS MOVES ARE ATOMIC EVENTS.	170
CONGRUENT RULE 49: THE KING ADVANCES TO ADJACENT TILES ON PERIMETER 1 VIA THE HIGHEST ADVANCEMENT RUNG.	187
CONGRUENT RULE 50: THE GAME IS OVER WHEN THERE IS NO LEGAL MOVE.	188
CONGRUENT RULE 51: IF NEITHER KING IS IN CHECK WHEN THE GAME IS OVER THE RESULT IS STALEMATE.	188
CONGRUENT RULE 52: A KING UNDER ATTACK IS IN CHECK, AND IT MUST GET OUT OF CHECK.	197
CONGRUENT RULE 53: IF A KING CANNOT GET OUT OF CHECK, THE GAME IS OVER, AND THAT PLAYER LOST.	197
CONGRUENT RULE 54: AN OPPOSING PAWN, ON THE NEXT MOVE ONLY, MAY CAPTURE A DOUBLY ADVANCED PAWN EN PASSANT BY MOVING TO A COVERED PATH TILE, IF ALL PATH TILES WERE COVERED BY ONE OR MORE PAWNS.	213
CONGRUENT RULE 55: CASTLING ALLOWED WHEN NEITHER KING NOR ROOK HAVE MOVED, AND WHEN THE KING DOES NOT MOVE OUT OF CHECK, INTO CHECK, OR THROUGH CHECK.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 56: CASTLING IS A SUPERNATURAL, ATOMIC EVENT, WHERE BOTH KING AND ROOK(S) MOVE SIMULTANEOUSLY, TYPICALLY IN THE SAME MANIFOLD.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 57: CASTLING MAY OCCUR IN ANY OR ALL ORTHOGONAL MANIFOLDS CONTAINING THE HOME LINE.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 58: THE KING CASTLES VIA A LINEAR ROOK MOVE TO PERIMETER 2.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 59: THE ROOK CASTLES TO ADJACENT TILES ON THE LEVEL ON THE FAR SIDE OF THE KING.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 60: AMBIGUOUS CASTLING IS INCLUSIVE; EITHER OR BOTH MAY BE CHOSEN.	235
CONGRUENT RULE 61: CAPTURE DURING CASTLING IS FORBIDDEN.	235
