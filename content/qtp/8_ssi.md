### Slope

The challenge is to find an equation that specifies the slope of the top edge of a world ribbon.
The principle of relativity requires that it have the same mathematical form in every reference frame.

### Photons

Consider a pair of EPR entangled photons headed in opposite directions:
one toward Alice, the other toward Bob.

Since Markdown files cannot easily represent equations or graphs,
the formal development is in PDF docs – see the SSI section for details.

One critical result from the formal development is that producing an entangled pair of photons
via spontaneous parametric down-conversion at a different harmonic (say the third) should 
not only yield photons with different frequencies but also create a substantial Poincaré rotation.

If true, it makes experimental validation of QTP much easier.
Since the slope specifies the spacetime coordinates of the cross pairs,
if false, the loop will require different spacetime coordinates in order to close.
Thus, short of the experimental demonstration of self-collapse, 
this remains a reasonable but speculative claim.

### The No Signaling Theorem

Given a symmetric interval between two observers, it is possible to determine
which measurement is the *cause* of the collapse and which is the *effect*.
This is an alternative to the ambiguous collapse paradigm.
However, it does not make any different predictions about experimental outcomes.
In particular, it cannot be used (at least not by itself) to send a superluminal signal.
Specifically, it does not violate the *no signaling theorem*.
So, while a symmetric interval is spacelike, and even has a 'causal' direction,
it is not a spacelike cause in the conventional sense.
A *choice* at spacetime event A does not cause an *outcome* at spacetime event B.
The causality is strictly a change in the wave function (a change in state)
from entangled to separated.

### Links for Self-Reference

However, what it does do is provide a potential link between spacetime events,
links that can be assembled into a closed loop in spacetime.

### The Diamond Paradox

Consider four SSI links, with carefully chosen Poincaré rotations, 
so they form a diamond shape on a spacetime diagram.
Four nodes (spacetime events) and four links (symmetric intervals).
Let each link consist of an EPR pair of photons.
Each pair is produced in an entangled state and allowed to propagate in opposite directions.
Pair propagation is unitary, with the symmetric intervals expanding as the photons propagate.
The desired Poincaré rotations could be achieved by placing the entanglement sources in rapid motion,
or by creating photon pairs at higher harmonics (different frequencies).

Each node receives a pair of photons from different EPR entanglements – the *cross pairs*.
Arrival times are coordinated so the cross pairs arrive at the designated spacetime coordinates.
Each arrival is a highly localized *spacetime event* – a *node*, if you will – 
intended to connect the entanglement *links* into a closed loop in spacetime.

### Causal Direction

A point to resolve is whether the causal directions of the links must all point the same way around the loop.

### Global Consistency

Now for the coup de grâce – the loop must be causally consistent.
Until the loop *closes*, the photons evolve unitarily.
Upon closure, however, the loop applies a *global consistency constraint*.
The hypothesis of QTP is that this constraint may not be consistent in all bases.

How to achieve loop closure is not trivial.
Linking the four entanglements into one cyclic entanglement
is a major theoretical and experimental challenge.
Addressing this challenge requires progress on the logical foundations of self-reference.

### Self-Collapse

If the constraint is satisfied in only one basis, then the loop self-collapses in that basis,
breaking all the entanglements – an objective measurement mechanism.
