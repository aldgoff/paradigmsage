### Conundrum

The nonlocal nature of quantum entanglement creates a quandry;
to affect something *over there*, spacelike separated from something *over here*,
invites suppositions about *spacelike causality*,
but spacelike causality is problematic.

Spacelike causality is required for FTL travel, even just for superluminal communication, but both are tantamount to *time travel*.

Given two spacelike links, it is possible to create a *closed loop in spacetime*, and that opens the door to *temporal paradox*.

If physics has taught us anything, it is that causality must be *consistent*;
traveling backwards in time to murder your grandfather before he met granny is causally ludicrous,
thus the modern conclusion that spacelike causality cannot be allowed.

### However

The introduction of imaginary truthvalues as a solution to logical paradox suggests that the causality objection is weaker than it appears. 

Building self-reference into logic allows the *Liar's Paradox*: "This statement is false"
to be given a logical value, actually a pair of them: *imaginary true* or *imaginary false*.
This is in perfect symmetry with the *Circular Argument*: "This statement is true",
which also has a pair of solutions: *Boolean true* or *Boolean false*.
They differ only by a *phase shift*.

Each self-referential form is *consistent* (indeterminate) in one set of logical values but *inconsistent* (paradoxical) in the other set - almost like *conjugate bases in physics*.

### Speculative Hypothesis

"A quantum system that evolves into a *cyclic entanglement*, around a *closed loop in spacetime*, 
self-collapses into the one basis in which it is causally consistent."

### Benefits

This would provide an *objective measurement mechanism*.
It would introduce *nonlinearity* into the quantum formalism *naturally*, via self-reference - no new physical constants.
The emergence of a closed causal loop imposes a *global consistency constraint* that
*selects* the collapse basis, with outcomes which are always *indeterminate*.
It resolves the annoying, artificial and arbitrary *division* of the world 
into a quantum system (treated at the quantum level)
and a measurement device (treated at the classical level),
which is of course, just a large quantum system.

### Challenge

What would have to be true for this hypothesis to be viable?
Responding to this challenge is the purpose of this website.

Spacelike causality might not only be allowed but *required*.
