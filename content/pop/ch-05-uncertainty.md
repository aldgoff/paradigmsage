**Chapter 5 - Uncertainty**

## Portent
  There is tension between observation and abstraction.
  Which one should dominant depends on where one is along the historical arc of a technical field.
  Sometimes more is less - and in perfect symmetry - sometimes less is more.

## Significance
  Too much information can create a mental box from which it is very hard to escape. 
  An abundance of observations can obscure patterns.
  Too many facts can bury hidden assumptions even deeper.

  The major **takeaway**: a paradigm shift may depend more on 
  ignoring (abstracting away all the dross) than on observation.
