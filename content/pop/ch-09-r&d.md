**Chapter 9 - R&D**

## Portent
  Risk. How do new theories come into existence?
  How does someone, anyone, let go of a successful past to chart a new and uncertain future?
  The lure of the quest; challenge, purpose, reward...and risk.
  Perhaps evolution has built this algorithm into us as well.

## Significance
  A scientific quest is a guess, a rational, reasoned guess about how reality might *be*.
  It is a mix of left brain and right brain activities.
  It requires inspiration as well as rigor.
  The motivation is in the pros, but the science is in the cons.
