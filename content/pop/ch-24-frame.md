**Chapter 24 - Frame**

## Portent
  The creative process; inspiration, progress, setback, breakthrough, catastrophe.
  It can be an emotional rollercoaster; it is not for the faint of heart.
  Science is a communal endeavour, we all stand on the shoulders of giants, 
  we all need a support network, friends, colleagues, students, opponents - even nemeses!

  Fortunate are those investigators who experience such a network.
