**Chapter 81 - The Ambiguous Collapse Paradigm**

## Portent
  Classical physics, commonsense, and 65 million years of evolution have conspsired to make us believe
  that effects have a cause (singular), or for the more sophisticated, a chain of causes. 
  In a good mystery novel, only one agent was the perp.
  When we design a game, each move has a single outcome, it is baked into the rules.
  Classical rules.

## Significance
  Quantum entanglement demonstrates spacelike *correlations*, but to have 
  spacelike *causality* requires one end to be a cause and the other to be an effect.
  Yet the claim of quantum mechanics is that both ends are cause, and both ends are effect.
  Design a game to those principles. 
  Try to explain those rules to even motivated players.
  Offer an explanation of what a move *is* under such ambiguity.
