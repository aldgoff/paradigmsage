**Chapter 8 - Quantum Temporal Paradox (QTP)**

## Portent
  Entanglement cannot be explained by timelike causality nor by common cause.
  But those are the only two types of causes we know about.
  Well, except for spacelike causality (and its equivalent common cause variation),
  but that can't be allowed - can it?
  If it is, the specter of causal paradox cannot be ignored.
  A choice must be made: 
  either assume it is insurmountable and thus a definitive barrier,
  or decide that it must be incorporated into physical theory.

  Is there any possible role for paradox in physics?

## Significance
  Self-reference and paradigms have led us down a primrose path.
  If all other solutions have failed, then perhaps (as Spock once said) an act of desperation can be a logical step.
  Wrestle with paradox, demand self-reference yield her secrets, push on reality till the *Aha* moment appears.
  Quantum Temporal Paradox (QTP) - a radical, speculative, hypothesis to solve the quantum measurement problem.
