---
title: "Paradigm Sage"
---

Quantum systems are counter-intuitive because they violate deep assumptions about how reality. 
The purpose of this site is to dismantle those assumptions in pursuit of a single long-standing conundrum: 
the *quantum measurement problem* — a mystery that has haunted physics for a century.

This exploration ranges across ideas that resist neat classification. 
It focusses on how constraints and invariants shape what is possible — 
in quantum physics, games, self-reference, logic, mathematics, computation, metaphysics, philosophy, and theology.

The work is organized as a set of projects that appear independent on the surface but are connected at a deeper structural level. 
The aim is not to offer premature answers, but to develop concepts and tools for thinking clearly in domains where the rules themselves are unstable. 
This is a work in progress; return from time to time to see what new territory has been mapped.

**Who this is for**: readers who enjoy sustained, careful thought about difficult ideas.

**Who it is not for**: those seeking quick explanations or light diversion.
