**Quick Start**

*A brief guide for those who like to dive right in.*

### Launch a Board
  Use the **Setup Panel** to make a board and place the pieces in their starting positions (two button clicks).

### Create an Advancement Square (advsq)
  Use the **Advsq Panel** to grow or shrink an advsq.
  - Use *Next Piece* to see how it differs across the base pieces.
  - Click on a tile and use *Place* to select its origin.
  - Explore the panel controls.
    - *Next Plane*
    - *Next Quad*
    - Stride

### Undo System
  Use the **Game Panel** to navigate the history.
  - Note there are four indpendent but sequential undo buffers.
  - Click on the help icon (on every panel) for more details.

### Change Point of View
  - Use the **Viewer Panel** to set the board into a slow oscillation around the vertical.
  - Use the **Camera Panel** to zoom in and out, or change the POV.

### Creating Gambits
  - Use the **Gambit Panel** to freeeze the current advsq.
  - Collect as many as needed to understand a situation.
  - There are multiple freeze options:
    - Quadrant
    - Linear
    - Duplex
    - Overlap
    - (the rest are in development)
  - Key affordances are used as a cognitive aids, they are dimmed when not eligible.

### Making Moves
  - Use the **Move Panel** to make moves, White then Black.
  - Rules are not yet enforced, just like with a physical board, that is the responsibility of the players.
  - To capture, click on two pieces, first captures second.
  - (Promotion and uplift are future features).
