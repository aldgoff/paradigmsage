title: "3D Chess"
type: "3dc"
---

This version of 3D chess uses *planar moves* and *advancement squares*.

The board has been promoted by a dimension, *so must the moves*. Instead of advancing in *straight lines*, pieces advance in *flat planes*.

This requires an expanded definition of *direction*, and the loss of the classical concept of *trajectory*.

No variation of the rules that insist on straight line moves can achieve play in 3D that feels like chess. 
The rules cannot be *invented*, they must be *discovered*.

This first version to be released to the public (0.6) meets the standards of *Minimal Viable Product* (MVP).
Much remains to be done, but it does provide enough tools for players to confirm if a move is legal.
