**Variations**

Present variations on QT3, such as
- N-way QT3
- Unitary evolution
- Probabilistic collapse
- Hybrid
- Indistinguishable QT3

## N-Way QT3
  There are two possibilities here: fixed N (like 3) and variable N (N>1).
  The simple cyclic entanglements of classical quantum tic-tac-toe (gotta love language)
  are no longer the only kind of entanglement that requires collapse.

## Unitary Evolution
  In this variation, no player gets to make a placement move.
  Spooky-marks are placed on the board by chance, or an algorithm, but not by choice.
  The only choice is that when a cyclic entanglement occurs 
  the appropriate player is required to make a collapse move.

## Probabilistic Collapse
  The inverse variation is to allow only placement moves, no player gets to make a collapse move.
  When a cyclic entanglement occurs, the collapse is random.
  This has a big impact on strategy.

## Hybrid
  In this variation the players are irrelevant.
  Placement moves and collapse moves just happen all by themselves.
  The statistics of wins and losses by X and O would be the interesting data points here.

## Indistinguishable QT3
  This is the most interesting variation, from a metaphor perspective.
  Game play, however, becomes problematic.
