## Reader Contract (Locked)

### RC‑1 — Single Promise

The site promises only a **serious, sustained pursuit** of the quantum measurement problem via paradox, self-reference, and explanatory coherence—not results or closure.

### RC‑2 — Narrative Sovereignty

**Paradigm of Paradox (PoP)** is the only guaranteed linear narrative at inception. Other topics begin as instrumental and may emancipate only after earning coherence and motivation.

### RC‑3 — Motivated Activation

No side topic becomes reader-facing before the PoP chapter that necessitates it. Premature exposure is non-authoritative.

### RC‑4 — Act Discipline

* **Act I**: establish problem space; recruit reader
* **Act II**: tools, objections, failures, stress tests
* **Act III**: consequences, byproducts, partial successes
  Rules:
* No Act II tool may function as Act I explanation
* Act III material may stand alone only once lineage is legible

### RC‑5 — Topic Lineage & Emancipation

Topics may emancipate into standalone research programs, but their origin in the PoP quest must remain discoverable.

### RC‑6 — Non‑Completion Signaling

Navigation, layout, and typography must never imply global completeness. Local completeness is allowed only within emancipated topics.

### RC‑7 — Reader Respect

Assume an intelligent, patient reader. Difficulty is managed through structure and staging—not dilution.
